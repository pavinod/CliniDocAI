# Situations

Patient: Anna Musterfrau, born 15 March 1959.

Type the title and description of a situation into your app. The questions are only for checking the screen. In every situation, your app should search all eight documents. Questions in the present tense, such as "Which drug does she take?", mean as of 15 April 2016, the date of the latest document. If something is not documented, the correct answer is "not documented". The expected answers are in [`expected.json`](expected.json).

## 1. Pre-operative anaesthesia check

You are the anaesthetist. She is scheduled for a quadrantectomy (partial removal of the breast) with intraoperative radiotherapy on 16 May 2016.

1. Which antithrombotic drug (blood thinner) does she take and at what dose?
2. Which allergy is documented?
3. What was the last documented left-ventricular ejection fraction (how much blood the heart pumps out with each beat)?

## 2. Surgery planning after the tumour board, April 2016

You are the surgeon planning her operation after the tumour board.

1. What is the histology (tissue type) and the TNM stage (tumour size, lymph nodes, spread)?
2. What is the receptor status and the Ki-67 rate (how fast the tumour cells divide)?
3. What did the tumour board recommend and when is it scheduled?

## 3. Diabetes review at the GP

You are her general practitioner (GP), reviewing her diabetes in spring 2016.

1. Which glucose-lowering drugs does she take and at what doses?
2. What is her latest HbA1c and from when?
3. How did her weight change between June 2015 and April 2016?

## 4. Emergency department at night

You are the emergency physician on night duty. She arrives at 02:00 with dizziness and blurred vision in the left eye.

1. Has this happened before and what was found to be the cause?
2. Is anything documented that matters before giving iodinated contrast (the dye for a CT scan of the blood vessels)?
3. Which antihypertensive (blood pressure drug) does she take and at what dose?

## 5. Open follow-ups from the 2015 hospital stay

You are her GP. You check which follow-ups from her June 2015 hospital stay are still open.

1. Which follow-ups were recommended at discharge in June 2015?
2. Is there a record that the follow-up carotid ultrasound took place?
3. On which side is the carotid stenosis?
